    About

    Cyberbullying has escalated with the rise of digital platforms, often manifesting through harmful images. Our project, "Image-Based Cyberbullying Detection Using Deep Learning," leverages advanced convolutional neural networks (CNNs) to identify and mitigate such content. By analyzing visual data, our system detects offensive imagery with high accuracy, offering a proactive solution to enhance online safety. We preprocess extensive image datasets, train models like ResNet and MobileNet, and compare their performance to ensure reliability. This initiative aims to protect users, promote a safer digital environment, and combat cyberbullying effectively through cutting-edge technology.
